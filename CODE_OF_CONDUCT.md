# Code of Conduct

KEYLIT is a tool for teaching beginners, and a lot of those beginners are children. The tone here should match that.

## What we expect

- **Assume the other person is learning.** That applies to piano and to code. Nobody arrives knowing this stuff.
- **Be specific instead of dismissive.** "This confused my daughter at step 3" is worth ten times "this is bad".
- **Take feedback on your own work the same way.** A review of your patch is not a review of you.
- **Say what you actually verified.** Overclaiming wastes someone else's afternoon.

## What is not welcome

Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments. Sexualised language or imagery — this is a project used by children. Publishing anyone's private information. Sustained disruption of discussions.

## If something goes wrong

Report it to the maintainer, [@uset82](https://github.com/uset82), through a GitHub message or a private issue. Reports are handled discreetly.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete comments and may block accounts. For anything serious, that will happen without a warning period.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, discussions, pull requests, commits — and anywhere someone is representing the project.
